🌱 Last Shelter

A sustainability-themed survival strategy game developed during a Game Jam.

🎮 About the Game

In this game, players must manage limited resources, protect their environment, and prepare for unexpected natural disasters. Every decision matters as players try to maintain a sustainable ecosystem and survive challenging conditions.

Disasters occur unexpectedly, requiring players to plan ahead, manage resources wisely, and adapt their strategies.

🌍 Gameplay & Features

Players can perform various actions to maintain their environment and survive:

🌾 Water Crops: Water the fields to help crops grow.

🌱 Harvest Crops: Collect grown crops and manage agricultural resources.

💧 Collect Water: Get water from the well.

☀️ Generate Energy: Store energy using solar panels.

♻️ Recycle: Recycle waste to support a cleaner environment.

🌳 Plant Trees: Plant trees to improve the environment.

⚠️ Natural Disasters

Two unexpected disasters occur each in-game day. Players must prepare for these events and make strategic decisions to minimize their impact.

🕹️ Objective

Your goal is to maintain a sustainable environment while managing resources and surviving unexpected disasters.

Think strategically, use your actions wisely, and keep your ecosystem alive!
